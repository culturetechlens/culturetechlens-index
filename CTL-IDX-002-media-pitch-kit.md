## The pitch in one paragraph

CultureTechLens NFP, a Chicago 501(c)(3) Black cultural-intelligence research
institution, publishes a monthly data-driven ranking — the CultureTechLens Index —
of cultural figures, moments, artifacts, and places in Black Chicago life. Every
score is computed from public attention data and CTL's own verified research
corpus; the full methodology and downloadable data ship with each edition. It is
designed to be cited: stable URLs, versioned editions, a public corrections log,
and a founder available for comment.

## Why it's citable (the hooks for editors)

- Transparent, published methodology (CTL-IDX-001) — the opposite of a hot take.
- Downloadable CSV of the full scored table with every edition.
- Stable per-edition URLs and a suggested citation string.
- A 501(c)(3) research institution, not a content farm — EIN 39-3143901.
- Monthly cadence = a recurring story slot, not a one-off.

## Editorial rules for Index publicity

- Never claim the Index measures importance, greatness, or worth — it measures resonance in the record.
- Never pitch it as a competition or a "who's the greatest" frame.
- Corrections policy is public; if we re-score, we say so.
- No outrage, tribal, or manufactured-urgency framing — per CTL editorial standards.
